GPU-waveMIS Ball Mapper — benchmark

three-way comparison (original / BallTree / GPU-waveMIS) · results host=gp5 · rendered 2026-06-30 19:14

GpuBallMapper replaces the CPU bottleneck — the sequential greedy landmark selection — with a parallel index-priority wavefront maximal-independent-set on the GPU, plus GEMM coverage and a cuSPARSE presence-only edge product. It is a drop-in replacement for FastBallMapper and produces a validated maximal ε-net (independently checked V1–V4).

Times are the median over repeats (robust to a one-off cold GPU rep). Validity is the independent V1–V4 check; GPU valid = OK means the GPU graph is a genuine Ball Mapper. FAISS is not part of this comparison.
